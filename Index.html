Nuclear Attack Submarine

Click any part for details • Drag to rotate
1. Pressure Hull
Thick steel cylinder that keeps the crew alive under extreme ocean pressure. Houses all living and working spaces.
2. Bow Sonar Dome
Large spherical acoustic array at the nose. Detects enemy ships and submarines from many kilometres away.
3. Sail (Fairwater)
The “tower” on top of the hull. Contains periscopes, masts, antennas and the bridge when surfaced.
4. Diving Planes
Horizontal control surfaces (bow & stern). Angle them to make the submarine dive or rise.
5. Rudder
Vertical fin at the stern. Turns the submarine left or right when underway.
6. Propeller (Screw)
Large multi-bladed propeller driven by the nuclear steam turbines. Provides silent propulsion.
7. Nuclear Reactor
Pressurised-water reactor that generates heat for steam. Allows the sub to stay submerged for months without refuelling.
8. Torpedo Tubes
Forward-facing launch tubes. Fire heavyweight torpedoes, cruise missiles or special forces swimmers.
